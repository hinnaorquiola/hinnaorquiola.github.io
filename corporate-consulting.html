04 / A considered process

“Clarity is not a shortcut. It is the starting point.”

STEP 01

Understand

Listen carefully, define the challenge, and establish what matters most.

STEP 02

Explore

Review the available information and identify realistic paths forward.

STEP 03

Shape

Organize recommendations into a clear, prioritized plan of action.

STEP 04

Move forward

Set practical next steps and ways to review progress over time.
